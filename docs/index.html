Neutral Desk

निष्पक्ष खबर, कई स्रोतों से जांची हुई. Neutral news, cross-checked across many sources.

Neutral Desk is a daily Hindi news video for India. Each episode reads reports from many Indian and foreign news outlets. Something is stated as fact only when at least three independently owned media groups report it. Claims from a single source are attributed to that source or marked as not independently confirmed.

The anchor, voice and script are AI-generated from public news reports. Every video links to all of its sources in the description.

This site belongs to a personal tool that uploads these videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.
