Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Neutral Desk is a personal automation tool. It uploads news videos to its owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, and only its owner signs in to it.

What the app accesses

The app asks for one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. It uses this permission only to upload videos, thumbnails and titles to the owner's channel. It does not read, collect or store any other data from Google accounts or YouTube.

How credentials are stored

The login token is stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's GitHub repository. It is used only by the owner's own scheduled job. It is never shared with or sold to anyone.

Data about viewers

The app collects no personal data from viewers or anyone else.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
